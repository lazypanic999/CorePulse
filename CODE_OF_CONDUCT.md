# Code of Conduct

CorePulse welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other project-managed spaces, as well as public interactions where someone represents CorePulse.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and considerate, especially with newcomers.
- Discuss the code and evidence. Give specific, constructive feedback and accept corrections.
- Respect different backgrounds, identities, accessibility needs, and communication styles.
- Credit other people's work and respect their privacy.
- Keep discussions relevant and give contributors room to participate without pressure.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory or sexualized language, threats, personal attacks, sustained disruption, spam, and publishing another person's private information without permission are not acceptable. Retaliation against someone who raises a concern is also prohibited.

## Reporting a concern

The project maintainer is [@lazypanic999](https://github.com/lazypanic999). To request a private reporting channel, open an issue titled **Code of Conduct: private contact requested** and tag the maintainer. Include only the contact request; do not publish names, incident details, screenshots, or personal information. The maintainer can coordinate a suitable private channel before you share the report. This project does not currently publish a dedicated conduct-reporting mailbox.

For abuse on GitHub, you can also use GitHub's built-in **Report content** or **Report abuse** controls. Security vulnerabilities belong in the separate [private security reporting process](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

The maintainer reviews concerns in context and limits sharing of report details to what is needed to investigate and respond. Depending on severity and repetition, responses may include requesting a correction, a warning, removing content, closing a discussion, or temporarily or permanently restricting participation. Serious threats or harassment may lead to an immediate restriction.

If you believe a moderation decision missed relevant context, request reconsideration through the same contact process. Reports involving the maintainer can also be directed to GitHub using its abuse-reporting controls; CorePulse currently has a single maintainer.
